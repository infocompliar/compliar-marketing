What's in an EICR

Everything an EICR needs to record, in a form that works on site

The Compliar EICR is built to record the information BS 7671 Part 6 asks for, in an order an engineer can work down on site.

Report details & supply

Purpose, extent and limitations, then supply characteristics and earthing: TN-S, TN-C-S, TT or IT, live conductors, external earth fault loop impedance (Ze), prospective fault current and the main protective device.

Observations

Each observation records the location, the finding, a classification code and a photo, and can be raised as a defect with one tick so it flows into quoting instead of being re-typed.

Inspection & test results

A schedule of inspections, then circuit by circuit: wiring type and reference method, protective device, r1 + r2, insulation resistance, polarity, Zs and RCD operating time.

Condition summary

An overall assessment of satisfactory or unsatisfactory, with notes on the general condition of the installation in terms of electrical safety.

Evidence & declaration

Photo evidence, a declaration, signatures and the recommended date of the next inspection, all on the one certificate.

EIC and minor works

The Electrical Installation Certificate shares the supply, inspection and circuit schedules for new work, and the Minor Electrical Installation Works Certificate covers small additions and alterations.

Beyond the form

FAQ

Common questions

Which electrical certificates does Compliar include?

The Electrical Installation Certificate (EIC), the Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) and the Minor Electrical Installation Works Certificate, each prepared to record the information BS 7671 Part 6 asks for. There are also templates for the other electrical disciplines: emergency lighting, PAT testing and EV charge points (including EV charger PPM).

Can I record circuit test results?

Yes. The schedule of circuit details and test results records the circuit description, wiring type, protective device, r1 + r2, insulation resistance, polarity, Zs and RCD operating time for each circuit.

Can I add photos to observations?

Yes. Each observation can carry a photo and a classification code, and can be raised as a defect so it goes straight into your quoting process.

Can I use my own certificate layout?

Yes. On Office seats and above, company admins can edit the templates or build your own, and every certificate is issued as a PDF with your company details.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The field app runs in the browser on a phone or tablet, so there is nothing to install. Once your jobs have loaded it keeps working with no signal on site and sends your work when you are back in range.

How much does it cost?

Compliar is priced per person. A Lite seat is £35 + VAT a month, Office £65 and Design £95, and certificates are included from the Lite seat up. Pay yearly and you get two months free. After a demo we can set you up with a 30-day free trial, with no card needed.
