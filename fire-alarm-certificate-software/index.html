The full BS 5839-1 certificate set

One template for each stage of a fire alarm system's life, each referenced to the relevant part of BS 5839-1:2025. Verification, modification or extension, standby power and battery record and schedule of test results templates are built in too.

On the certificate

Built for the way engineers actually fill them in

Commissioning

Commissioning scope, system operational checks, interfaces and alarm transmission, then documentation, false alarms and any outstanding work, all in sections you can work down in order.

Servicing visits

Checks for every visit and the annual inspection, panel, PSU and battery readings, photo evidence and a recorded outcome. Add the standby battery calculator when you edit the template.

Your own templates

Edit the templates or build your own (company admins, Office seats and above), so a certificate matches how your company works rather than how a software vendor thinks it should. The built-in templates are included from Lite.

From site to customer

The certificate is one step in the job, not a separate chore

  • Required-certificate checklist. Each job lists the certificates the engineer needs to complete before sign-off, so nothing is forgotten.
  • Photos and signatures on the device. Evidence and sign-off are captured as part of filling the certificate in, on a phone, tablet or desktop.
  • Branded PDF, emailed from the job. The certificate is issued as a PDF with your company details and sent to the customer from Compliar, with a record of what was sent.
  • Defects become quotes. A fault found on a servicing visit can be raised as a defect and quoted without re-typing, then tracked as an open defect.
  • History and customer access. Issued certificates are kept per site, and a customer portal login can show them as soon as they are issued.

FAQ

Common questions

What certificates are there for a fire alarm system under BS 5839-1?

BS 5839-1 provides for a certificate at each stage: design, installation, commissioning and acceptance or handover, with routine servicing recorded on an inspection and servicing certificate. Compliar has a template for each of these, referenced to the relevant part of BS 5839-1:2025. Always check what your contract or specification requires on top.

Can I edit the certificate templates?

Yes. On Office seats and above, company admins can edit the templates that come with Compliar or build your own, so you are not waiting on a software vendor to change a form. The built-in templates are included from Lite.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The field app runs in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to install from an app store. Once your jobs have loaded it keeps working with no signal on site and sends your work when you are back in range.

Does it calculate the standby battery?

Yes. A BS 5839-1 Annex E standby battery calculator is available in the certificate builder (company admins, Office seats and above) when you build or edit a template, so you can add it next to the panel, PSU and battery readings. The same method is on our free fire alarm battery calculator.

How much does it cost?

Compliar is priced per person. A Lite seat is £35 + VAT a month, Office £65 and Design £95, and certificates are included from the Lite seat up. Pay yearly and you get two months free. After a demo we can set you up with a 30-day free trial, with no card needed.
