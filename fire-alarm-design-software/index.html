What you get

Drawings that sit alongside your jobs.

As-fitted plans and zones

The Design editor draws floor plans with walls and doors, zones and as-fitted plans. Load an existing plan with Upload Plan, then place devices on it.

Drawings can be exported with a title block that carries your company logo.

  • As-fitted plans, with deviations from the design noted on the drawing
  • Rooms labelled with their floor area

Symbols and cable runs

A symbol catalogue and a drawing key sit beside the plan. There are extra symbol sets for power, lighting, CCTV, intruder, door entry and fire equipment, and you can upload your own symbols.

Cable runs can be drawn with a cable type, size and colour, with exact segment entry, angle steps and snaps.

Take-offs from the drawing

The Flow to BOQ button takes the drawing through to a bill of quantities, so quantities come from what is on the plan.

Take-offs are part of the Design seat, which is £95 per user per month + VAT (or £950 a year), and includes everything in Office.

Then carry the job on

Once the system is fitted, the same site carries the maintenance plan, the log book and the certificates, so the drawing, the visits and the records stay together.

Questions

Common questions

Who is the Design seat for?

Engineers and project teams who draw fire alarm layouts. It is £95 per user per month plus VAT (£950 a year) and includes everything in Office.

Can I upload my own plan?

Yes. Use Upload Plan in the Design editor to bring in a plan, then place devices on it.

Can I use my own symbols?

Yes. Your company can upload custom symbols, and there are extra symbol sets for power, lighting, CCTV, intruder, door entry and fire equipment.

Does it produce take-offs?

Yes. Flow to BOQ takes the drawing to a bill of quantities.

Does the drawing make the design compliant?

No. Compliar is a drawing and records tool. The designer and the scheme remain responsible for the design.

Can I try it?

Book a demo and we will show the Design editor on your kind of site.
