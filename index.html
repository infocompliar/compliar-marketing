Standby Battery Size Calculator.

The real compliance calculation engine from Compliar’s servicing and commissioning certificates. Calculate minimum required standby capacity (Cmin), check the 20-hour discharge derating factor (D), and get the next standard VRLA battery size up.

Minimum required capacity (Cmin) 6.71 Ah

Formula & working

Cmin = 1.25 × (T1 · I1 + D · I2 · T2) = 1.25 × (24 × 0.180 + 1.75 × 1.200 × 0.50) = 6.71 Ah

20-hour discharge check: Underrated capacity C1 / 20 = 0.25 A < I2 (1.20 A), so Annex E.1.2 derating factor D = 1.75 is applied, plus the 1.25 (25%) battery ageing allowance.

Built on Site, Not in a Boardroom

Created by an Engineer Who Lived the Problem Every Day.

Compliar started because field service software in the fire, security, and electrical trades felt like it was designed by accountants who had never stepped foot on a plant room riser or tested an emergency light fitting.

Founded by Keiran Sandham, Compliar was built directly from the tools up. After years of chasing lost defect remedials, wrestling with clunky mobile apps that crash when signal drops, and typing the same certificate details three times over, Keiran built Compliar to give independent contractors and busy service desks software they could actually take pride in using.

We don't lock you into multi-year contracts, and we don't hide features behind enterprise paywalls. We just build software that keeps you compliant with British Standards, wins you more defect work, and lets your engineers get home on time.
