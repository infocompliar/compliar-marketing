Four things an auditor will notice

  • Issued certificates can never be deleted Every edit to an issued certificate needs a reason, and the reason is logged in the job's event log with who made it and when.
  • Every device is an asset with its own test history Each detector, luminaire, camera and circuit is a record on the site, so the last result and the one before it are a tap away.
  • Each site shows red, amber or green from real records The colour comes from failed assets, open defects and overdue maintenance, not from a tick box. Your office is alerted when a site turns red, and your customers see the same status on their portal map.
  • Instruments fill in on the certificate The equipment register holds each tester with its serial number, calibration date and who holds it. The engineer's instruments fill in on the certificate, and calibration due dates are tracked.

Digital QR log book and stickers

A QR sticker on the panel replaces the paper log book.

The site contact scans the sticker, signs in and logs the weekly fire alarm test from the phone. Thirteen record types, from weekly tests and false alarms to building alterations and fire risk assessments, are kept against the site. Missed tests send an email to the contacts you set, and a link on the log book requests a call-out.

  • £4 + VAT per site per year Add log books to any plan, or charge your maintenance clients for them at your own price. A log-book-only account is available for anyone who holds sites.
  • Stickers £1 + VAT each QR log book tags in white or your brand colour, false alarm notices and front-of-panel service stickers, posted for £3.95 + VAT per order. Or print them yourself on the label printer you already have.
