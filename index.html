About Us · Authentic UK Trade Roots

Founded by Keiran Sandham from Inside the UK Fire, Security & Electrical Trade.

Compliar comes from the UK electrical, fire protection and life safety, and security industry, not from a generalist software studio guessing how site engineering works.

Founded by Keiran Sandham, Compliar replaces fragmented spreadsheets and paper binders with one system built around the way electrical, fire protection and life safety, and security contractors work, with templates that reference standards such as BS 7671 and BS 5839.

Whether your engineers are testing a 4-loop addressable fire panel in a plant room with zero signal, completing an EICR, or printing a replacement QR log book sticker on site, every screen in Compliar is designed to get the certificate signed, the remedial quoted, and the engineer home on time.

Keiran Sandham Founder of Compliar · Fire & Security Manager · 20+ years in UK electrical, fire & security contracting. Day to day: operations, design, surveying, quoting, technical support, hiring, buying and sales. Read the full Compliar story →

Why UK Contractors Switch to Compliar

  • No Per-Certificate Fees Certificates are included with every seat, from Lite up. You pay per user per month, not per certificate.
  • Records Kept Together Certificates, asset history, false alarm investigations and digital QR log books are held against each site, so they are easy to find when your scheme or a customer asks.
  • No Contract Early adopters who sign up in the first 12 months after launch lock their price for 3 years. Onboarding is from £195, quoted individually.

Compliar provides record-keeping and certificate workflows. It doesn't certify work or grant accreditation, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by BAFE, NSI, SSAIB, NICEIC or BSI. Your competent engineers and scheme remain responsible for the work and certificates issued.
