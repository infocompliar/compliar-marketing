CERTIFICATES & STANDARDS

Certificates built to follow the standard.

See certificates & reports ↗

Every certificate template is built around the standard for its discipline, and each one names the standard it follows. Where a trade works to a code of practice or regulation rather than a British Standard, such as the IET Code of Practice for in-service inspection and testing, the template names that instead. Each certificate is stored against the job and site, and downloads as a PDF. The engineer who signs it remains responsible for its content.

All FAQs ↗
Can the office and engineers use different plans?

Yes. Mix Lite, Office and Design seats in one team. Choose the tools each role needs, with no minimum number of seats.

How do the QR code fire safety logbooks work?

The site log book is £4 + VAT per site per year, added to any plan, or taken on its own with no seats. Create a user-customised, printable QR label for the site. When your team scan it, they sign in and open that site’s logbook in the app. The logbook is not part of the customer portal.

How do I get started?

Request a demo and we will walk through your own jobs. If Compliar fits, we set up your account and a 30-day free trial, with no card required. We can also help with set-up and moving your data across if you would like it.
