Disciplines & British Standards Taxonomy

Every discipline cleanly grouped by trade sector.

Engineered around real UK contracting divisions — Electrical Installation & Testing (EIC, EICR, MEIWC, PAT) stays strictly in Electrical, while Fire, Emergency Lighting, Security, and Passive Fire each have their own dedicated British Standard schedules.

Sector A

A. Electrical Installation & Testing

Complete BS 7671:2018+A4:2026 schedules of circuit details, test results, observation codes (C1, C2, C3, FI, N/V), and appliance registers.

EICBS 7671:2018+A4:2026

Electrical Installation Certificate — New installations, consumer unit upgrades, and circuit additions with design, construction & inspection sign-offs.

EICRBS 7671:2018+A4:2026

Electrical Installation Condition Report / Fixed Wire — Periodic inspection schedules, C1/C2/C3/FI observations, PME/PNB earthing & distribution boards.

MEIWCBS 7671:2018+A4:2026

Minor Electrical Installation Works Certificate — Additions and alterations to an existing circuit without a new circuit.

PATIET Code of Practice 5th Ed

In-Service Inspection & Testing of Electrical Equipment — Class I / Class II appliance registers, earth continuity, insulation resistance & PASS/FAIL barcode tags.

EV ChargingIET / BS 7671

Electric Vehicle Charging Equipment Installation — O-PEN device verification, DNO notification records, RCD test times & load curtailment checks.

Solar PV & Battery StorageMCS / BS 7671

EESS & Grid-Tied Inverter Systems — DC string open-circuit voltage, short-circuit current, AC isolator verification & G98/G99 commissioning data.

Sector B

B. Fire Detection & Alarm Systems

Full Annex G certificates, zone/loop asset matrices, standby battery sizing verification, and clause 14.22 false alarm tracking.

Commercial Fire AlarmsBS 5839-1

Design, Commissioning, Acceptance, Modification & Servicing — Complete Category L/M/P certificates, cause & effect matrices, and 100% annual device schedules.

Domestic Smoke & Heat AlarmsBS 5839-6

Grade D/F Installation & Testing — Landlord, HMO, and residential fire detection commissioning and periodic inspection records.

Voice Alarm Systems (PA/VA)BS 5839-8

Periodic Servicing & Intelligibility — Speech transmission index (STI/STIPA) readings, emergency microphone tests & amplifier changeovers.

False Alarm ManagementBS 5839-1 cl 14.22

Cause, Investigation & Audit Reports — Unwanted fire signal (UwFS) root-cause tracking, category logging, and active ARC connection panel notices.

Sector C

C. Emergency Lighting

Monthly functional flick tests, annual 3-hour full discharge schedules, lux level verification, and new design completion sign-offs.

Periodic Inspection & 3-Hour Discharge TestBS 5266-1 / BS EN 1838

Complete Luminaire Schedules — Fitting-by-fitting location, maintained/non-maintained mode, battery duration pass/fail & instant FAILED sticker printing.

Completion & Commissioning CertificatesBS 5266-1

New Design Sign-Offs — Escape route, open area & high-risk task area illumination declarations and photometric design verification.

Sector D

D. Security, CCTV & Access Control

Structured for NSI Gold / Silver and SSAIB audited contractors across intruder, monitored CCTV, access control, gates, and healthcare call systems.

Video Surveillance Systems (CCTV)BS EN 62676 / BS 8418

Commissioning & Maintenance — Camera field-of-view schedules, NVR retention checks, detector-activated RVRC signalling & privacy masking audits.

Intruder & Hold-Up AlarmsBS EN 50131 / PD 6662

Grade 2/3/4 Systems — Zone walk-test schedules, dual-path signalling verification (URN), power supply load checks & maintenance certificates.

Access Control SystemsBS EN 60839

Door Controllers & Readers — Fail-safe fire alarm interface drop-out testing, maglock holding force checks, and break-glass release audits.

Automated Gates & BarriersDHF TS 011 / BS EN 12453

Force Testing & Safety Audits — Closing force measurements, photocell/safety edge actuation, manual release & crush/entrapment risk checks.

Nurse Call & Assistance AlarmsHTM 08-03

Call Points, Panic Buttons & Overdoors — Pull-cord, call unit, display panel, and cardiac/emergency override verification for care homes and clinics.

Sector E

E. Passive Fire & Suppression

Asset-level extinguisher registers, damper drop-test photos, riser pressure readings, AOV smoke vents, and 20+ point fire door inspections.

Fire ExtinguishersBS 5306-3 & BS 5306-8

Commissioning, Annual & Extended Service — Weight, pressure gauge, discharge horn, wall bracket, and 5/10-year extended service/overhaul tracking.

Fire DampersBS 9999

Drop Testing & Ductwork Inspection — Fusible link/motorised actuator release, damper blade closure photos, cleaning & reset confirmation.

Dry & Wet RisersBS 9990

Visual & Annual Pressure Testing — 6-monthly visual cabinet checks and annual 10 bar / 12 bar hydrostatic pressure test certificates.

Smoke Control & AOV SystemsBS 7346

Routine Actuation & Maintenance — Automatic opening vents, stairwell smoke shafts, fireman’s override switches & battery backup checks.

Fire Door InspectionBS 8214

20+ Point Compliance Check & Gap Gauges — Head/stile/threshold gap measurements (2–4 mm), intumescent/cold smoke seals, hinges, glazing & self-closers.
