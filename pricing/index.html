QR stickers for your sites.

Order blank QR stickers for £1 each + VAT (orders of 10 to 1,000, in tens), which you activate onto a site by scanning, or printed stickers made out to a named site for £4 each + VAT. Postage is £3.95 + VAT per order for either. Or print your own QR codes for free from the app, in many label sizes from 40 × 30 mm up to A5, including Brother VC-500W colour label rolls.

False alarm notice stickers can be bought in bulk for £1 each + postage (£1 + VAT each, £3.95 + VAT postage per order), printed with your contact number for the fire alarm panel. Or print them yourself for free from the app.

Printing your own? We recommend the Brother VC-500W colour label printer, but any printer that takes labels or sticker paper will do.

ONBOARDING

Help getting started, quoted for your business.

Onboarding is hands-on help to get you set up and working in Compliar. It is open to everyone, whatever plan you choose and however many seats you have, including a single Lite seat. You don't need any experience with this kind of software. It is separate from the seat prices. Onboarding fees start from £195. Every onboarding is quoted individually, so the price is fair for your business and agreed before anything starts.

  • Setting up your account, users and seats, and showing you around
  • Bringing across your customers, sites and assets from your current system
  • Setting up your certificate templates and job types
  • Getting your engineers started on the field app

Exactly what is included depends on your team and your current setup. The quote sets it out in writing. Prefer to do it yourself? After your demo, a self-service setup is available to everyone.
