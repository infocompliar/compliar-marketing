Cookies, and
your choice.

This website only sets analytics and advertising cookies if you choose “Accept”. Here is what each choice means, how to change it, and what the Compliar application stores.

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

compliar.co.uk

On your first visit a banner asks whether you accept optional cookies. You can change your choice at any time with (also in the footer of every page).

Essential (always on)

We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage under the name compliar_cookie_consent, so we don't ask again on every page. It is not used for anything else.

Analytics and marketing (only if you choose “Accept”)

We use the Google tag for Google Ads to measure which adverts lead to demo requests. If you accept, Google may set cookies such as _gcl_au on compliar.co.uk (kept for up to 90 days) and its own cookies on Google domains such as doubleclick.net. Google acts as an independent controller for this data; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you choose “Decline”, or make no choice, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. We use Google Consent Mode, so the Google tag still sends Google limited, cookieless signals (for example that a page was viewed or a demo button was clicked, with your IP address and browser details as part of the request) so that conversions can be modelled in aggregate.

Other third parties

Typefaces load from Google Fonts, and Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies, without storing anything in your browser and without tracking you across sites; see our Privacy notice.

The Compliar application (app.compliar.co.uk)

The application uses only strictly necessary storage: a session token so you stay signed in, local storage for drafts and preferences such as the last label size you printed, and a copy of your work records and unsent changes kept on the phone (in IndexedDB, with a service worker) so the field app keeps working without signal. These are essential to provide the service you asked for, so consent is not required for them and there is no banner. None of it is used for tracking. The application sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment pages when you enter card details; see Stripe's privacy policy.

Managing storage

Use Cookie settings on any page to change your choice on compliar.co.uk. You can also clear site data for compliar.co.uk and app.compliar.co.uk in your browser settings at any time. Clearing it for the application signs you out and removes unsent drafts.

Questions

Email enquiry@compliar.co.uk. Our Privacy notice explains the personal data we hold.
