Legal · Cookies

No cookies
on this site.

This website sets no cookies and loads no advertising or tracking scripts. It uses cookie-free page counting. Here is what that means in practice, and what the Compliar application does differently.

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

compliar.co.uk

The pages on this site are static. They do not set cookies, do not store anything in your browser and do not include pixels or embedded content that could track you. The pages make two third-party requests: typefaces from Google Fonts, and Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies, without storing anything in your browser and without tracking you across sites; see our Privacy notice. If we ever add analytics that uses cookies we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

The Compliar application (app.compliar.co.uk)

The application uses only strictly necessary storage: a session token so you stay signed in, local storage for drafts and preferences such as the last label size you printed, and a copy of your work records and unsent changes kept on the phone (in IndexedDB, with a service worker) so the field app keeps working without signal. These are essential to provide the service you asked for, so consent is not required for them and there is no banner. None of it is used for tracking. The application sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its payment pages when you enter card details; see Stripe's privacy policy.

Managing storage

You can clear site data for compliar.co.uk and app.compliar.co.uk in your browser settings at any time. Clearing it signs you out and removes unsent drafts.
