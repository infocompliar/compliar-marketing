Questions

Common questions

How long does the early-adopter price last?

Companies that join in the first 12 months after launch keep their seat prices for three years from the date they joined.

Is there a contract?

No. There is no minimum term and no minimum number of seats. You can cancel from the billing settings at any time, and access continues to the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I join?

Book a demo. Keiran walks through Compliar on your own jobs and sets your company up himself.

What happens to my price after three years?

The three-year lock means the seat prices you joined at cannot go up for three years. After that, any change to the published prices applies with notice, as set out in the terms, and you can still leave at any time.

Can I add or remove seats during the lock?

Yes. Seats are mixed and changed as your team changes. Added seats are charged pro rata and keep your locked price per seat type.

What does it cost?

Lite is £35, Office £65 and Design £95 per user per month plus VAT, or ten months for the year if you pay yearly. A Lite seat paid yearly works out at about £1 a day.
