Fire detection and alarm maintenance · compliance

Fire detection and alarm maintenance and compliance software for contractors and their engineers.

Plan the visits, send the engineer out with an app that works offline, issue the certificate, and turn defects into quotes. Compliance alerts show which sites need attention.

What you get

From the maintenance plan to the certificate and the quote.

Plan the work on the calendar

Maintenance plans and routines create the visits, and the calendar shows them by engineer. Maintenance plans are in the Office seat.

The Jobs to plan list has Map and Rounds buttons. Tick jobs to plan a route, drag them to an engineer and preview the order before you schedule them. Travel times are estimates.

An offline field app with a visit tracker

Engineers see today’s jobs on their phone. The app keeps today’s work on the phone, so it keeps working when the signal drops, and queued changes send when it is back.

Each visit has a five-step tracker from Arrive to Complete, with the site’s open defects shown on the job.

  • Navigate and On my way buttons on the job
  • Pass, fail or no-test recorded on tap for the assets in a visit

Certificates from over 100 templates

Over 100 certificate and form templates across 32 system types in three disciplines. Fire alarm templates reference BS 5839 and the engineer fills them in on site, collects signatures and emails the PDF to the customer.

The app also has a standby battery calculator. You can try the demonstration calculator on this site.

  • Fire detection and alarm certificates
  • Battery calculator

Defects become quotes

Defects found on site are listed on the job, with a Needs quoting status. They go into a defect quote, and the customer can approve a quote from a link in the email.

The Quotes and Tenders list filters by status and type, with a Defect tab, and downloads as CSV.

Compliance alerts on the Home page

The Home dashboard lists the sites that have turned red, each with its failed assets and critical defects, so the office can see what needs action first.

Screens show sample data. Compliar produces records; the engineer and the scheme remain responsible for the work and for the certificates issued. Compliar is not approved by or affiliated with any standards body or scheme.

Questions

Common questions

Does it work without a signal?

The field app keeps reads on the phone and queues changes, so engineers can keep working in a plant room with no signal. Changes send when the phone is back online.

Which seat do I need?

Lite (£35) covers the calendar, jobs, defects, certificates, quotes and the field app. Office (£65) adds maintenance plans, invoicing and more. Prices are per user per month plus VAT.

Does it include fire detection and alarm certificates?

Yes. There are over 100 certificate and form templates, with fire alarm templates that reference BS 5839. The engineer and the scheme remain responsible for the work and the certificates.

Can it connect to Xero?

Yes. Compliar can push invoices to Xero and bring payments back.

Is there a contract?

No. Early adopters who join by 31 October 2027 lock their price for 3 years.
