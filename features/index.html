Design & take-offs

Turn the drawing into the starting point.

Create CAD drawings, take off quantities and price the work from those quantities. Take-offs and CAD drawings are included with Design. Design tools are in beta.

What’s included

Office seats include projects, tenders and pricing from your quantities. Design seats include everything in Office, plus take-offs, CAD drawings, zone plans, as-fitted plans, wiring diagrams and schematics for your installation and handover records.

  • CAD drawings for design and installation records
  • Take-offs and pricing from your quantities, on Design (Design tools are in beta)
  • Zone plans, as-fitted plans, wiring and schematics
  • Symbol sets for fire, power, lighting, CCTV, access, intruder, extinguishers and data
  • Staircases and landings drawn to scale, internal and external
  • Measure distances and cable runs from the drawing scale, and keep them as dimension lines
  • Choose the brand, protocol and product for each device before turning the take-off into a quote
  • Import supplier price lists with brand, protocol and device type

Fire safety logbooks

The site’s record, beyond the installation.

Keep a digital fire safety logbook within Compliar. Give ongoing site records a home alongside certificates, defects and equipment. Print a user-customised QR label for the site so your team can find its logbook.

What’s included

Enable a digital logbook for the site, with 13 record categories (including weekly alarm tests, emergency lighting tests, false alarms, fire drills and fire doors), kept by your team in the app so the record remains useful after the job is handed over. Create a customised QR label and print it for display at the site; your team scan it, sign in and open that site’s logbook.

  • Fire safety logbooks within the app, £4 + VAT per site per year (added to any plan, or as a log book only account with no seats, for any business that looks after several properties; UK only, working to British Standards)
  • Site-based records in 13 categories
  • User-customised, printable QR labels for the site’s logbook
  • Kept by your team in the app; not part of the customer portal
RAMS

Plan safer work for everyone on site.

Build risk assessments and method statements around the job. Set out hazards and working methods to help protect engineers, clients and others on site.

What’s included

Create a RAMS straight from a job and it pulls in hazards and a method statement from ready-made templates covering every discipline Compliar supports — or start from a blank RAMS for anything bespoke. Review and adapt the hazards and methods to the actual site and task, then sign off and issue as a PDF. RAMS is included on every seat.

  • Task-linked creation, auto-populated from the site and job
  • Ready-made templates across every discipline, or build one from scratch
  • Editable hazards, electronic sign-off and a printable PDF
Scheduling

See every maintenance visit before it's due

Plan maintenance visits by property, see the whole schedule and reassign work as plans change.

What’s included

Day, week and month views of every maintenance plan, tied to the property it's scheduled against — so a visit never quietly slips past its service interval.

  • Maintenance visits scheduled per property, not per technician's memory
  • Combined calendar for both office planning and field work
  • Reschedule and reassign without leaving the job
Trades & disciplines

Only the trades and disciplines you actually do

Fire protection and life safety, security and electrical. Cover the full job: design, pricing, installation, testing, commissioning, handover and maintenance. Choose your disciplines to keep irrelevant forms and equipment out of your team’s way.

What’s included

Tell Compliar which trades you cover — fire protection and life safety, electrical, security — then switch whole trades or single disciplines on and off, and drop the job types you don't service, such as aspirating, risers or suppression. Compliar hides the forms, job templates and equipment options that don't apply, on the desk and in the field.

  • Switch each trade on or off for your company
  • Switch whole trades or single disciplines on and off, along with the lines of work you don't run
  • One-click presets, such as core systems only, to get started fast
Fire risk assessments

FRAs are part of the workspace.

Carry out fire risk assessments with built-in risk scoring (scored from likelihood and consequence). Keep assessments and reports alongside the site’s certificates, defects and fire safety records.

Bring the assessment, remedial work and ongoing maintenance into the same workflow, with RAMS for planning the work safely.

Certificates & reports

The paperwork for every trade. Connected to the job.

Complete installation, commissioning, inspection, testing and maintenance certificates and reports for every discipline we support, across fire, security and electrical. Built-in calculations, site records and your own templates keep the details together.

What’s included

Certificates and reports for every discipline we support — 21 fire protection and life safety disciplines, 7 security disciplines and 5 electrical disciplines, from fire alarms and emergency lighting to CCTV, door entry, EICRs, PAT testing and EV charge points. The full list is under Certificates & standards. It also includes two built-in calculators most platforms leave to a separate spreadsheet: BS 5839-1:2025 Annex E battery sizing, available when you build or edit a template, and fire risk scoring.

  • Build and edit your own certificate templates — no waiting on anyone else (Office seats and above)
  • Fire-risk scoring calculated as you fill the form, and a BS 5839-1 Annex E battery calculator you can add when building or editing a template
  • Clean, read-only print view and a full issued-certificate history
Site compliance

Every site, red, amber or green — at a glance

See which sites need attention, with status based on failed assets, open defects and overdue maintenance.

What’s included

Compliar scores each site from its real records — failed assets, open defects and maintenance plans past due — and takes the worst of its systems. The colour shows on the dashboard, the sites list and every customer, and your office team gets an in-app alert when a site turns red.

  • Red for failed assets, critical defects or overdue servicing; amber for other open defects or servicing due within 30 days
  • A customer's colour is the worst of its active sites, with the sites that need attention listed first
  • Scored from the work your engineers actually record — no separate spreadsheet to keep up to date
Quotes & invoices

Sales work and defects, kept separate

Keep sales quotes and remedial work organised, then move from accepted work to invoicing.

What’s included

New business and remedial work found on a maintenance visit run through two distinct quote types, each with its own numbering sequence — so your win-rate reporting on one doesn't get muddied by the other. Every quote, invoice and certificate dispatches as a real branded email with the PDF attached — not a pre-filled draft you have to send yourself.

  • Sales quotes and defect quotes, tracked and numbered separately
  • Real email dispatch straight from the job, PDF attached automatically
  • Company bank details and terms pulled in automatically
  • Quotes and defect quotes start on a Lite seat; invoicing and credit control are on Office seats and up, along with recurring contract invoices, deposits, progress claims, CIS and retention
Xero integration

Invoices and payments, linked with Xero

Compliar links with Xero in both directions, so you don't type invoices or payments in twice.

What’s included

Connect your Xero organisation from Settings. Send an invoice in Compliar and it is created in Xero, matched to the customer's Xero contact (or a new contact if there is none). When a payment is recorded in Xero, the Compliar invoice is marked paid or part paid. Payments come back automatically, and Sync now brings them in straight away.

  • Sending an invoice creates it in Xero, with the customer's contact matched or created
  • Payments recorded in Xero mark the Compliar invoice paid or part paid, automatically
  • Invoices already in Xero are locked in Compliar: edit them in Xero or raise a credit note
  • VAT codes map to Xero tax rates; CIS and retention are handled
  • Connected per company, from Settings
Handover packs

A proper handover. A clearer record.

Bring the handover story together: certificates, zone plans and as-fitted drawings that show the customer what was installed.

What’s included

Drawings on Design seats cover zone plans, as-fitted plans, wiring and schematics for your handover documentation. Every job also carries its own documents card: the job report, an asset report, each certificate, every quote and every invoice, all as branded PDFs. View or download any of them, tick the ones you want to send and email just those — Compliar keeps track of what has gone out.

  • View or download any document in one click, straight from the job
  • Tick what to send — only those are emailed and marked as sent
  • A sent / not sent marker on every document, so nothing is chased twice or forgotten
Field app

Everything a technician needs, on site

Record time, parts, notes and certificates from your phone. The field app keeps working with no signal once your jobs have loaded on the phone, and sends your changes when you are back in range.

What’s included

A travelling → on-site status with its own timer, one-tap directions to the next stop, parts used, billable line items, a required-certificate checklist and technician notes — one simplified mobile view, with none of the admin clutter of the desk side.

  • Travelling / on-site status with an automatic timer, plus one-tap directions
  • Parts used and billable items logged from the van
  • Required-certificate checklist, so nothing's forgotten before sign-off
  • Engineers see the full job and site history, never quotes; or limit them to their assigned jobs
Defects

From failed asset to fixed, without re-typing

Turn a failed asset into a defect, quote and follow-up job without entering the same details again.

What’s included

Fail an asset on site and the defect is raised against it. Pick the wording from your own library of remarks, apply the same remark to every selected asset in one go, then turn the defects into a quote in a click and see at a glance which still need one, which are quoted and which are resolved.

  • A remark library you control, so the same fault reads the same way on every report
  • Apply one remark to many assets at once
  • Quote the selected defects in one click, linked back to the asset that failed
  • Suggest a quote straight from a failed asset test, with parts marked for the quote or charged on the visit
  • Part suggestions match the device type, brand and protocol being replaced
Job costing

Know what every job really earned

Track labour, parts and revenue to see what each job costs and the margin it earns.

What’s included

Log labour on the job from the desk or the phone, billable or not, with a cost rate and a charge rate for each engineer. Every job shows its sell revenue against labour and materials cost, with gross profit and margin worked out as you go.

  • Cost and charge rates per person, set once in Settings
  • Parts used on the job costed from your own stock prices
  • Margin shown on every job, colour-coded, so thin ones stand out
Projects & tenders

Price a whole project, area by area

With an Office seat, build project estimates by area, with parts, labour, waste and your target margin. Add take-offs from your drawings with a Design seat. Design tools are in beta.

What’s included

Start a project from a prospect or an existing customer site, break it into floors and areas, and build the bill of quantities line by line — parts, labour hours and waste — at your target margin. Cost, sell, margin and VAT update as you build it.

  • Projects are numbered and kept apart from your day-to-day jobs
  • Areas seeded from a template, then edited to suit the building
  • Parts and labour priced from your own rates, with live margin
Assets, stock & settings

The record of what's actually installed

Keep equipment records and stock organised, with an asset register for each property.

What’s included

A per-property asset register, parts and warehouse stock levels, purchase orders dispatched straight to suppliers, and company-wide settings for branding, document numbering and terms — the groundwork every other part of Compliar relies on.

  • Full equipment register per property, in your own asset types with codes like FAP-001
  • Parts, warehouses and live stock levels
  • Purchase orders, dispatched to suppliers with a real PDF attached
  • The asset register starts on a Lite seat; stock and purchase orders are on Office seats and up
Reporting & insights

What's due, what's overdue, and where the revenue is

See maintenance status, outstanding invoices and revenue trends without building another spreadsheet.

What’s included

Compliance status and data-quality checks across your whole customer base, plus a financial view (on Office seats and up) — revenue trend, outstanding invoices and win rate — without exporting anything to a spreadsheet first.

  • Compliance status — overdue, due-soon and on-track maintenance plans, at a glance
  • Data-quality checks — properties with no maintenance plan, no assets or no contract
  • Revenue trend, outstanding invoices and win rate (Office seats and up)
Customer portal

No more chasing PDFs by email

Give customers access to certificates, defects and equipment. Invite a contact for the whole account or a single site.

What’s included

A client-facing login, with each contact invited for the whole account or a single site, showing the customer their certificates, open defects and live equipment.

  • Invite each contact for the whole account or a single site
  • Certificates, defects and equipment visible the moment they're issued
  • A map of every site, coloured by compliance status
