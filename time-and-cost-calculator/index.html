How to read the result

The time figure is simply certificates per month multiplied by the minutes you expect to save on each, at your hourly cost. If you think Compliar will take longer per certificate than what you do now, enter that and the figure goes negative; the calculator does not argue. The subscription line is the published seat prices for the mix you pick, ex VAT. The optional "what you pay now" line adds back whatever software or per-certificate fees Compliar would replace.
