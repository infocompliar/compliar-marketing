Paper, spreadsheets or software: what each costs a small contractor

A plain comparison for small and medium contractors: what paper, spreadsheets, per-certificate software and a one-app system really cost in money and time.

Illustration only. The figures below use Compliar's published prices (ex VAT) and example numbers you should replace with your own. Last reviewed 2 November 2026.

The short version

A small contractor can run on paper, on spreadsheets and email, on software that charges for every certificate, or on software that charges per user. The cost is not just the subscription. It is also the evenings spent typing up, the certificates re-done because a field was missed, and the quote that never went out because the defect was only written in a notebook.

What per-certificate pricing does to a busy month

Per-certificate pricing looks cheap in a quiet month and expensive in a busy one, which is the wrong way round for a growing firm. Compliar charges per user and nothing per certificate. An illustration with published prices:

  • 3 engineers on Lite: 3 × £35 = £105 a month, ex VAT, billed monthly. Billed yearly it is £350 per user a year, so £1,050 for the three.
  • Add one office user: an Office seat is £65 a month, or £650 a year.
  • Log books: £4 per site per year, only for sites you put a digital log book on.

Whether that beats your current spend depends on your own numbers: how many certificates you issue, what you pay for them now, and how long each takes to write up. Put your own figures into the time and cost calculator. It does the sum on what you type in and does not assume a saving for you.

Where the time goes

  • Writing up after the visit. If results are recorded on site, even with no signal, there is nothing to type up in the evening.
  • Chasing missing fields. A certificate that will not issue until the required readings are in is quicker than finding out at the office.
  • Defects that get lost. A failed item logged on the visit can go straight into a quote instead of being remembered on the drive home.
  • Reminders and renewals. Maintenance plans that generate the next visits save keeping a diary of due dates.
  • Invoicing. A completed job can go to invoice without re-entering the customer, site and work done.

Keeping records you can stand behind

Cost is only half of it. Customers, insurers and assessors ask for the history of a site: what was tested, what failed, what was done about it. Certificates laid out to the standards (BS 7671 for electrical, BS 5839-1 for fire detection and alarm systems, BS 5266-1 for emergency lighting, the BS EN 50131 series for intruder and hold-up alarm systems) and a log book that stays with the site keep that history in one place. Compliar provides the record-keeping and certificate workflow. It does not certify work, and your competent engineers and scheme remain responsible for what is signed.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it cheaper than paying per certificate?

It depends on how many certificates you issue and what you pay now. Compliar charges per user with no per-certificate fee, so the cost does not rise in a busy month. Use the time and cost calculator with your own numbers.

Do I still need spreadsheets if I use Compliar?

Not for jobs, certificates, quotes, invoices, log books or drawings, which all sit in the one system. You can import your existing customers and sites, and export your data.

Does Compliar certify my work?

No. Compliar provides record-keeping and certificate workflows. Your competent engineers and your scheme remain responsible for the work and for what is signed.

Is there a contract?

No contract. Early adopters lock their price for 3 years.

Who will I speak to?

The founder, Keiran Sandham, who has 20+ years in UK electrical, fire and security contracting, runs the demos.
