Good to know

What PAT testing involves, in short

PAT is the in-service inspection and testing of portable electrical equipment. There is no single legal test interval. How often an item is inspected and tested depends on the type of equipment, how it is used and the environment, so a laptop in an office is treated differently from a power tool on a building site.

A visual inspection comes first, because most faults can be seen. Where the equipment type and your risk assessment call for it, electrical tests follow, such as earth continuity and insulation resistance for Class I equipment. Class II (double insulated) equipment is generally tested differently, so record the class against the item.

Whatever you decide for a site, the useful thing is a dated record per appliance that shows the result and when it is next due. The IET Code of Practice for In-service Inspection and Testing of Electrical Equipment is the usual reference. Check it, and your own competent-person judgement, for the job in front of you. Compliar keeps the records; it does not decide the test regime for you.

FAQ

Common questions

Is there a fixed interval for PAT testing?

No. The interval depends on the type of equipment, how it is used and where, and is set from a risk assessment. The IET Code of Practice for In-service Inspection and Testing of Electrical Equipment gives guidance. Always check it for the job in front of you.

Can I record PAT results on my phone?

Yes. The field app runs in your phone's browser with nothing to install, and once your jobs have loaded it keeps working with no signal on site and sends your work when you are back in range.

What happens when an appliance fails?

You can raise it as a defect from the failed result, and it can be quoted for repair or replacement, then tracked as an open defect against the site.

Can I print PAT labels?

Each appliance can carry a printed QR asset tag (50 × 25 mm, printed from the site to a Niimbot, Phomemo, Brother or Dymo label printer), and scanning it on a later visit opens that item. Pass and fail result labels are planned; ask at the demo for the current position.

Is PAT included in every plan?

Yes. The built-in certificate and report templates are included from the Lite seat at £35 + VAT per user per month, with no per-certificate fees and no contract.
