A few useful answers.

Read all FAQs ↗
How do I get started?

Request a demo and we will walk through your own jobs. If Compliar fits, we set up your account and a 30-day free trial, with no card required. We can also help with set-up and moving your data across if you would like it.

What if I am moving from another system?

Before moving your records, talk to us about your current setup, the information you want to bring across and any help you need. We can discuss what is available for your business, including any separately quoted onboarding.

Can I use it on site?

The field app runs in your phone’s browser. Once your jobs have loaded on the phone it keeps working with no signal: your jobs, certificates and times are saved on the phone and sent when you are back in range.

How do I get help?

Email enquiry@compliar.co.uk with your company name and question. You can also arrange a walkthrough to discuss your workflow, seat choices and setup questions.
